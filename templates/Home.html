About Linklet

Who Are We?

Founded in 2020 by a team of cybersecurity experts and authentication specialists, Linklet emerged from a simple yet powerful vision: to eliminate the friction and security risks of traditional password-based authentication.

🎯

Our Mission

To make authentication seamless, secure, and accessible for everyone. We believe that security shouldn't come at the cost of user experience.

🚀

Our Vision

A world where passwords are obsolete, and every digital interaction is protected by intelligent, frictionless authentication.

How It Works

Experience the magic of passwordless authentication in three simple steps

1

User Enters Email

User provides their email address on your login form

2

Magic Link Sent

Linklet generates a secure, time-limited magic link and sends it via email

3

Instant Login

User clicks the link and is instantly authenticated - no passwords needed!

⚡

Lightning Fast

Authentication happens in milliseconds. Our AI engine processes risk factors in real-time, ensuring both speed and security without compromise.

🔒

Bank-Level Security

Each magic link is cryptographically signed, time-limited, and single-use. Our AI continuously monitors for suspicious patterns and anomalies.
